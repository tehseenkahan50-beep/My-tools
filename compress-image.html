Compress Image

Make photos smaller for email, forms and uploads.

    How to use it

    1. Choose one or more photos.
    2. Tap Convert.
    3. Download the smaller JPG files. The size before and after is shown.

    About this tool

    Compress Image shrinks large photos to at most 2000 pixels on the longest side and saves them as JPG at balanced quality. Most photos get much smaller with little visible change.

    Questions

    Which formats work?

    You can pick JPG, PNG or WebP. The result is always a JPG.

    Does quality drop?

    Slightly. Photos stay sharp on screens, but this is not lossless.

    What happens to transparent PNGs?

    Transparent areas turn white, because JPG has no transparency.
