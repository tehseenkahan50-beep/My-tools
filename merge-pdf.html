Merge PDF

Join two or more PDF files into one document.

    How to use it

    1. Choose at least two PDF files.
    2. Files are joined in the order you picked them.
    3. Tap Convert, then Download the merged PDF.

    About this tool

    Merge PDF joins files page by page, so text and images keep their quality. It is useful for combining forms, invoices or chapters into a single file.

    Questions

    How do I change the order?

    Files are joined in the order you pick them. To change it, reload the page and pick them again in the order you want.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not yet. Remove the password first, then merge.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser.
