About FileFlip

FileFlip is a set of free file tools for PDF, images and Excel. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device. There is no sign-up and no watermark.

Tools

  • JPG to PDF
  • Merge PDF
  • Compress image
  • Excel to CSV
  • Word to PDF
  • PDF to Word
  • Scan to Word (OCR)
