Word to PDF Converter

Convert a DOCX document into a PDF.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your DOCX file.
    2. Tap Convert.
    3. Download the PDF and check it before sharing.

    About this tool

    Word to PDF reads your DOCX file in the browser and builds a PDF from it. Headings, bold text, lists, tables and images are kept. Complex layouts such as columns, text boxes and special fonts may look different.

    Questions

    Does it work with .doc files?

    Only .docx. In Word, save older .doc files as .docx first.

    Can I select text in the PDF?

    Not in this version. The pages are saved as images. If you need selectable text, use Save as PDF in Word.

    Is my document uploaded?

    No. The conversion runs on your device.
