Excel to CSV Converter

Turn an Excel spreadsheet into CSV files.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your XLSX or XLS file.
    2. Tap Convert.
    3. Download the CSV. Each sheet becomes its own file.

    About this tool

    Excel to CSV reads your spreadsheet in the browser and writes each sheet as a separate CSV file. CSV opens in almost any tool, which helps with imports, databases and data analysis.

    Questions

    What about files with many sheets?

    Each sheet is saved as its own CSV file.

    Are formulas kept?

    CSV stores the calculated values, not the formulas.

    Is my spreadsheet uploaded?

    No. It is read on your device and never sent anywhere.
